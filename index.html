Selected Work

Rewriting the Vision-Language Bridge: Fast Closed-Form Backdoor Editing for Multimodal Connectors

First-author manuscript in preparation, 2026

Proposed a training-free closed-form editing method for visual connectors in multimodal large language models. The method formulates targeted backdoor editing as a constrained linear rewrite in connector mapping space, with stealthy visual triggers and Fisher-discriminant-based column gating to reduce semantic leakage.

In experiments on LLaVA-v1.5-7B, the method achieved about 92% targeted induction success rate while reducing false trigger rate from about 37% to about 18%.

Enterprise Multi-Agent Collaboration and Task Governance

Research internship, Fudan Microelectronics Group, 2026

Designed a general agent-to-agent collaboration framework for enterprise multi-user scenarios. The system models supervisor, peer, assistant, and collaborator relationships as a directed relation graph, and upgrades natural-language handoff into structured envelopes with task IDs, source and target agents, constraints, completion criteria, and evidence packets.

On DDR-Benchmark 10-K tasks, the A2A system reached 76.48% message-wise accuracy and 53.66% trajectory/chat-wise accuracy.

DialecticFlow: A Controllable Debate LLM Agent for Reliable Long-Form Interaction

Project lead, 2025-2026

Built a Qwen2.5-7B-based debate agent with staged LoRA training, stance-aware RAG, dual-track memory, post-generation checking, and diagnostic rewriting for Chinese debate scenarios.

The full system improved normalized internal evaluation score from 29.5 to 94.0 across logic, rebuttal precision, stance consistency, closing quality, and AI compliance.

Two-Stage Diffusion Purification for Backdoor Defense

Patent, student contributor 2, 2024-2025

Contributed to a black-box input-side purification framework for image-classification backdoor defense. The method combines global lightweight DDPM perturbation, SSIM-based trigger localization, and local differentiated purification.

Reduced attack success rate from over 90% to below 6.15% on CIFAR-10/GTSRB while maintaining at least 90% clean accuracy. Chinese invention patent 202511807335.9 is under substantive examination.

Embodied Drawing Robot with SylixOS and EtherCAT

National innovation project, 2025-2026

Built an embodied drawing system connecting image generation, sketch conversion, trajectory planning, real-time industrial communication, and six-axis robot execution. I worked on porting the EtherCAT master stack to SylixOS, driver adaptation, IOCTL command reconstruction, and 1 ms cyclic servo communication.

The project won National Third Prize in the Operating System Design Track of the National College Computer System Capability Competition.
